
# Purchase to Payment

## Flow

Purchase Requisition
→ PR Approval
→ Purchase Order
→ PO Approval
→ Purchase Receive
→ Supplier Bill
→ Accounting

## Expected Relationships

Approved PR can be converted to PO.

Approved PO can be received.

Purchase Receive updates inventory.

Supplier Bill is based on received quantity.

Accounting entries must remain balanced.

Debit = Credit
